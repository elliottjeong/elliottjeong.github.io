Sketching

01

A progressive sketching practice

A curriculum moving from form construction to complete marker-rendered layouts, followed by an independent chair study focused on more organic forms.

  1. 01 · Curriculum Basic sketch Lines, boxes, circles, and combined forms
  2. 02 · Curriculum Layout sketch Multiple views, details, and functions
  3. 03 · Curriculum Marker rendering Early color, value, and material exercises
  4. 04 · Curriculum Integrated rendering Marker color applied to complete layouts
  5. 05 · Independent study Chair exploration Organic form developed for a personal use case

01–04 Curriculum progression 05 Independent continuation

Stage 01 · Curriculum foundation

Basic sketch

Foundational exercises for controlling lines and building boxes, circles, rounded volumes, and product-like forms in perspective.

Stage 02 · Curriculum development

Layout sketch

Object studies arranged as explanatory pages, using multiple views, details, functional sequences, and small annotations to communicate how a product works.

Stage 03 · Curriculum rendering

Marker rendering

Early marker exercises testing color, value, highlights, shadows, and material cues on simple product volumes before moving into full layouts.

Stage 04 · Curriculum completion

Marker-rendered layouts

The final curriculum stage combines the explanatory composition of layout sketching with marker color, value, and material rendering.

Stage 05 · Independent continuation

Chair exploration

After completing the curriculum, I applied the same process to a chair for my own use. Because chairs rely on more organic surfaces, transitions, and ergonomic relationships, this study pushed form construction and detail further through repeated variations.
