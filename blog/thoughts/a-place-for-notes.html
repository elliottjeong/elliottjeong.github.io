A place for notes

A small new room for keeping track of ideas before, during, and after they become work.

This page is a starting point. It can hold a project update, a material experiment, a reference that keeps returning, or a record of a question in progress.

Each note lives on its own page, while the Notes page keeps a short archive of every entry. As the archive grows, newer writing can be placed at the top.

To publish another note, duplicate this file, change its title, date, and text, then add a matching entry to its collection and the recent-updates list.
