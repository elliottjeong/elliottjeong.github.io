Who am I

I follow wherever my curiosity leads me.

That has taken me through art, 3D and product design, computer science, and engineering—and somehow into robotics, with a few unexpected detours through physics and chemistry. I like robotics. There’s no single lane, and it doesn’t put me in one box. I get to use all of me, and there’s always more to learn.

It probably doesn’t look like a straight path from the outside. To me, it is. People sometimes ask me what the end goal is, probably because I tend to work as if there must be one. The truth is, I don’t really have one. I’m less goal-driven than stimulation-driven.

Some people go looking for a drink. I go looking for something new to learn, build, or get a little too obsessed with. Building is one of my favorite forms of distraction. And I need a lot of distractions. Give me something I care enough about finishing, and I’ll learn whatever it takes to get there.
