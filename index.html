Hello

I’m Eunah Jeong.

I studied art, digital media, 3D and game design, computer science, and nano science and engineering. I worked as a furniture designer, but somehow fell into robotics—then, by a twist of fate, found myself suffering through electromagnetic theory and physical chemistry to make my way into the field.

My path may not sound linear or consistent, but it all makes sense to me. I follow the things that give me the greatest stimulation and curiosity. Learning and building are the perfect distractions for me.

I need a lot of distractions to survive, so I work hard—especially when I am given something I can become obsessed with completing. I’ll learn anything if it takes me where I want to be.
